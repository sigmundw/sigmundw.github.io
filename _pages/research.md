My research connects **Arctic hydroclimate, water-balance change, and climate impact attribution** to understand how Alaska’s hydrologic response changes under climate warming.

## Current research

<div class="research-feature research-feature--alaska">
  <p class="profile-card__eyebrow">University at Buffalo · A.C.T. Hydrology Lab</p>
  <h3>Historical and projected changes in Alaska’s runoff-to-precipitation ratio</h3>
  <p>I investigated historical and projected changes in Alaska’s water balance and runoff-to-precipitation ratio (R/P) using 4-km coupled land–atmosphere simulation data. I applied a Budyko-based water-energy balance framework to attribute R/P changes to precipitation supply, atmospheric evaporative demand, terrestrial water storage, and effective basin characteristics.</p>
  <p><strong>Advisor:</strong> <a href="https://arts-sciences.buffalo.edu/earth-sciences/faculty-staff/faculty/cheng-yifan.html">Dr. Yifan Cheng</a></p>
</div>
